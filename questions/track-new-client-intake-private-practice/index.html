How do I track new clients from first inquiry to first session?

Give each new client one row, by ID code, with a status (waitlist, intake, active) and the date the status last changed. Note the referral source, the intake date and the dates the paperwork came back, such as consent and your cancellation policy. A quick look then shows who's stuck in intake, what's still missing, and how many active clients you have before you say yes to someone new.

How to set it up

  1. Use an ID code, not a name, and keep contact details wherever you already store client information securely.
  2. Record the referral source on the first call or email. After a few months it shows where your clients actually come from.
  3. Use a small set of statuses, such as waitlist, intake, active and discharged, and write down the date each time one changes. That date is what tells you someone has been in intake too long.
  4. Track paperwork with dates, not checkmarks: consent signed, cancellation policy signed, and card on file if you keep one. A blank date is the thing to chase before the first session.
  5. If the client is using insurance, note the payer, and any authorization the payer requires, before the first session.
  6. Count your active caseload before you offer a slot, so you only take on as many clients as you have time for.
  7. Change the status when a client starts or finishes, and keep the row instead of deleting it, so the history stays with your records.

Intake records can include protected information. Keep names, contact details and clinical notes in a secure system, and follow your licensing board's and your own practice's rules on records and privacy. Check with each payer about what it needs before a first session.

Common follow-up questions

How long should intake take?

There's no standard number. Writing down the dates for a few months shows you your own typical time, and which step usually holds things up.

When should a new client count as active?

That's your call, so pick one rule and stick to it. One simple rule is to switch a client to active once the first session is held, so the active count reflects people you're actually seeing.

What if a new client goes quiet during intake?

Follow up, note the dates, and if you don't hear back, close the row out with a status instead of deleting it. That keeps your count of inquiries honest.
