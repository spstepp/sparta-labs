How do I track CE credits and license renewals for every clinician in a group practice?

Keep one list with a row for each clinician and each license they hold: state, license type, license number, expiry date, CE hours required, hours done and hours left. Sort it by expiry date, or flag anyone due in the next 90, 60 or 30 days. Keep certificates in one shared folder with a subfolder for each clinician, and decide who sends the reminders. Requirements differ by board and state, so take each license's numbers from that board's own rules.

How to set it up

  1. Make one row per clinician per license. A clinician with two state licenses and a national certification gets three rows. Fill in the name, state, license type, license number, expiry date, hours required, hours done and hours left.
  2. Take the requirements from each board's own website for each state and license type. Note total hours, required topics such as ethics, and any limits on online or self-study hours. Two clinicians with different licenses may have different rules.
  3. Sort by expiry date so the next renewal is at the top, or add a flag for anyone due within 90, 60 or 30 days.
  4. Keep certificates in one shared folder, with a subfolder named for each clinician. Ask clinicians to save each certificate there when they finish a course, and mark it as filed on the list.
  5. Name one person who owns the reminders. Decide who checks the list each month and who tells a clinician they're short on hours or close to the deadline.
  6. Update the row after each renewal with the new expiry date, and start the hours count over for the new period.
  7. Check the list when someone joins or leaves, so a new hire's licenses are added and former staff come off.

CE hours, required topics and renewal cycles differ by board, state and license type, and can change between cycles. This is record-keeping guidance, not legal or licensing advice. Check each board's current rules for every license on your list.

Common follow-up questions

Should clinicians track their own CE, or should the practice?

Either can work, as long as it's clear who does what. One way to split it: each clinician logs their courses and saves certificates, and one person checks the team list each month and sends reminders.

How far ahead should we flag a renewal?

Early enough for someone who is short to find and finish a course. Flags at 90, 60 and 30 days give you three chances to catch it.

What should go in the shared certificate folder?

The certificate for each course, saved under the clinician's name, with a file name that shows the date and course title. Keep them as long as each board says it may ask for them in an audit.
