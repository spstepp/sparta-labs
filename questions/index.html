Practice questions

Authorization visits, unpaid claims, CE hours, missed-session fees, RBT supervision: the record-keeping that piles up in a solo practice. Keep each one straight, by hand or with a spreadsheet, so a deadline doesn't sneak up on you.

  • How do I track how many insurance authorization visits a client has left?
    Insurance authorization tracking for therapists: see visits left and end dates for every client, and get the next request in before either runs out.
  • What should a therapy insurance claims tracker include?
    Date of service, denial reason, days outstanding and the other columns that show which insurance claims to chase.
  • How do I track CE credits for license renewal?
    Track continuing education hours, required categories and certificates for one or more licenses, and check your totals before renewal comes around.
  • How do ABA providers track authorization units?
    Track authorized units by client and CPT code: units used, units scheduled, units left, and whether you're on pace.
  • How do I track no-show and late-cancel fees in private practice?
    Log missed sessions, apply the fee from your written policy, and keep track of what was collected, waived or reduced.
  • How do I track RBT supervision hours each month?
    Compare each RBT's supervision time with their service hours for the month, and spot a shortfall while there's still time to fix it.
  • How do therapists in private practice track quarterly estimated taxes?
    Set money aside for quarterly estimated taxes, track what you paid each period, and see a shortfall before the due date.
  • How do I keep track of a therapy waitlist in private practice?
    Keep a waitlist: who's waiting, what they need, when you last contacted them, and who to call first when a slot opens.
  • When should ABA providers start a reauthorization request?
    Plan each reauthorization request by working back from the payer's lead time, scheduling the reassessment, and watching end dates and unit pace.
  • How do I track denied insurance claims in a small practice?
    Log denied claims, record the denial reason, follow up on resubmissions and see which payers deny most.
  • How do therapists track income and expenses in private practice?
    Log income by source, sort expenses by tax category, check a monthly profit number and match it to the bank.
  • How do ABA practices track RBT and BCBA credential expiration dates?
    Keep every RBT and BCBA certification date in one list, with warnings early enough to finish renewal before anything lapses.
  • How do therapists keep track of what clients owe?
    Track session charges, payments and unpaid balances by client, grouped by how old they are, so nothing owed gets forgotten.
  • How do therapists keep track of sliding scale fees?
    Record each sliding-scale client's agreed fee, log what was charged and paid, and review reduced fees on a set schedule.
  • How do therapists reconcile their practice records with the bank statement?
    Check that income, insurance payments and expenses in your records match the business bank statement, once a month.
  • How do counselors track CE hours when they're licensed in more than one state?
    If you're licensed in more than one state, track each board's CE hours, required topics and renewal dates in one place.
  • How do ABA providers track parent training (97156) units against the authorization?
    Keep caregiver training units under CPT 97156 separate from direct therapy, see what's used and booked, and catch units going unused before the authorization ends.
  • How do therapists make a monthly profit and loss statement in private practice?
    Build a monthly profit and loss: income by source, expenses by category, net profit, and a 12-month view, without mixing in transfers.
  • How do ABA providers catch sessions that don't match an authorization?
    Flag sessions with no matching authorization, the wrong CPT code, or a date outside the authorized period, before they are billed.
  • How do therapists catch insurance underpayments?
    Compare each insurance payment with the expected rate and flag claims that were paid short.
  • How do I get ready for a CE audit from my licensing board?
    Match every CE course to a certificate, count only courses inside the renewal period, and check category hours before an audit.
  • How should therapists categorize business expenses for taxes?
    Give every business expense a tax-ready category, mark personal spending, and hand your CPA sorted records instead of a pile of receipts.
  • How do I track new clients from first inquiry to first session?
    See where each new client stands between first contact and first session: status, referral source, paperwork dates and caseload count.
  • How do I get my private practice books ready for my CPA at tax time?
    Income received, expenses by category, tax set-aside totals and a year that matches the bank, ready for your CPA.
  • How do I track where my therapy clients come from?
    Log the referral source for every new inquiry, so you can see which directories, doctors and colleagues actually send you clients who stay.
  • How much should a self-employed therapist set aside for taxes?
    Work out a tax set-aside percentage from your profit and last year's return, then check it each quarter so April isn't a surprise.
  • How do therapists track business mileage for taxes?
    Log business trips with the date, miles and purpose, leave out commuting, and turn the total into one expense your CPA can use.
  • How do I record card payments when the processor takes a fee?
    Log the full client payment as income and the processor's cut as an expense, so your books match your bank and any 1099-K.
  • How do therapists track home office expenses for taxes?
    Measure the space, keep the year's home bills, and log the deduction as one expense once your CPA picks a method.
  • How do I record insurance payments from an EOB or ERA without counting them twice?
    Post each insurance payment once, against the claim it pays, and use the EOB to check the amount before you call it done.
  • How do I track RBT direct service hours each month?
    Total each RBT's direct service hours by month from completed sessions, so supervision and authorization numbers start from the same count.
  • How do I calculate my practice's insurance claim denial rate?
    Work out your own claim denial rate from a claims log, then look at it by payer and by reason to see what to fix.
  • Do therapists in private practice have to pay quarterly estimated taxes?
    Most therapists in private practice pay estimated tax four times a year. The IRS rule, the 2026 due dates, and how to keep track.

These pages are general record-keeping guidance, not billing, legal or clinical advice. Payer contracts and board rules vary, so check with your payer or licensing board.
