How do I check that a CE course will count for my license renewal?

Check with your licensing board before you take the course, not after. Look at three things: whether the board accepts that provider or approval, whether it limits the format (online or self-study hours, for example), and whether the course covers a required topic you still need, like ethics. A national approval doesn't settle it on its own. ASWB says that even where a social work board accepts its ACE approval, the board has the final say on any course, and New York doesn't accept ACE approval at all.

How to check a course

  1. Start with your board's CE rules page. Note which providers or approvals it accepts, any cap on online or self-study hours, and the topics it requires.
  2. Check the provider's approval before you pay. Look for the approval statement and provider number on the course page, then confirm it on the approving body's own site. For social work, ASWB has an ACE provider search.
  3. Pick courses that fill what you're still short on. If you have enough total hours but no ethics hours, an ethics course does more for you than another general one.
  4. Make sure you'll finish inside your current renewal period, with the certificate in hand, so the hours count this cycle.
  5. If you hold more than one license, check each board. A course one board accepts may not count for another.
  6. Write down the approval when you log the course, with the provider and approval number, and save the certificate the same day.
  7. If you're not sure, ask the board before you take it. It's quicker than finding out at renewal.

Which providers, formats and topics count is up to each licensing board and can change between renewal cycles. This is record-keeping guidance, not legal or licensing advice. Check your board's current CE rules before you rely on a course.

Common follow-up questions

Which CE courses are best for meeting a renewal deadline?

The ones your board accepts that cover what you're still missing, and that you can finish, with a certificate, inside your current period. Fill required topics first, then total hours.

Does an ACE or other national approval mean every board will accept the course?

No. ASWB says most U.S. and Canadian social work boards accept ACE courses, but each board has final authority over any course, and New York doesn't accept ACE approval. For other professions, check your own board's list of accepted approvals.

What if I already took a course and I'm not sure it counts?

Ask your board, and don't count the hours until you know. Keep the certificate and the course description in case the board wants to see them.
