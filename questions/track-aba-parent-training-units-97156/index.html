How do ABA providers track parent training (97156) units against the authorization?

Treat family or caregiver training as its own pool of units. Keep a separate authorization row for 97156 with the units approved and the dates, log each training session under that code, and compare units used with how much of the period has passed. Parent training is easy to fall behind on, so a pace check shows early when units are likely to go unused.

How to set it up

  1. Give 97156 its own authorization row, separate from direct treatment (97153) and protocol modification (97155). Each code is usually its own pool of units.
  2. Log each caregiver session under 97156 with the date, units and the BCBA who ran it. Use your payer's rule for turning minutes into units.
  3. Count completed sessions as used and booked ones as reserved. Cancelled sessions count toward neither.
  4. Check the pace every couple of weeks. If half the period has passed and only a small share of the training units are used, talk with the family about scheduling before the authorization ends.
  5. Flag sessions that don't match an authorization, such as the wrong code or a date outside the authorized period, before they're billed.
  6. Bring the numbers to reassessment. How many training units were used, and how often sessions were cancelled, helps when you plan the next request.

Whether 97156 needs its own authorization, how units are counted, and whether the client must be present all depend on the payer and plan. Check your payer's policy and follow your organization's billing rules.

Common follow-up questions

Is parent training usually authorized separately from direct therapy?

Often it's approved as its own code with its own units, but payers differ. Read each authorization letter to see how the units are split.

What if parent training units keep going unused?

Look at the pattern first: cancellations, scheduling conflicts, or sessions that were held but logged under the wrong code. A pace check every couple of weeks gives you time to fix it before the period ends.

Can I track group caregiver training (97157) the same way?

Yes. Give 97157 its own authorization row and log those sessions under that code, so the two pools don't get mixed.
