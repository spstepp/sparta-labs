How do ABA practices track RBT and BCBA credential expiration dates?

Keep one list of every staff member by ID code, with their role and the date their certification or credential expires. Work out days left from today, and set two warnings: an early one with time to plan the renewal steps, and an urgent one. Check the list every month, and update the date as soon as a renewal goes through.

How to set it up

  1. List every staff member who needs a credential, by ID code (for example RBT-3), with their role and the expiration date shown on their certification record.
  2. Find out what renewal involves for each role. Check the BACB's current handbook for RBT and BCBA renewal steps and timing, and check any state license your staff also hold. Renewal rules can change, so look again each year.
  3. Work out days left from today's date, so the list stays current without retyping.
  4. Set an early warning and an urgent warning, for example 60 and 30 days. Pick the early one so there's room for the slowest step, such as booking an assessment or finishing required training.
  5. Review the list once a month, soonest date first, and give each person a heads-up when they reach the early window.
  6. Update the date the day a renewal is confirmed. An old date left in place keeps showing a warning and hides the next real one.

Certification and renewal requirements are set by the BACB, your state and your employer, and they can change. Check the BACB's current handbook and your state's rules before you rely on any date or window.

Common follow-up questions

Can I track CPR cards or background checks too?

Our tracker holds one credential date per staff code, so use it for the certification that matters most for billing and supervision. Other dates need their own list, or another spreadsheet column you add yourself.

Should staff names go in the spreadsheet?

We suggest staff ID codes, with the key that links codes to names kept somewhere separate. That keeps the file simpler to share with the people who need it.

How early should we start an RBT renewal?

Early enough to finish every step before the expiration date. The BACB sets when some steps can be done, so check its current handbook and set your early warning to match.
