Therapist Caseload & Insurance Authorization Tracker

For Excel and Google Sheets

A spreadsheet for solo therapists and small practices who bill insurance. It tracks each authorization's visits left and warns you before one runs out or expires. It also logs no-show and late-cancel fees, ages unpaid balances, and keeps your waitlist with days since last contact. Clients are entered by ID code only. There is no place for clinical notes.

  • Insurance authorizations: visits used, visits left, and alerts before the end date
  • No-show and late-cancel fees
  • Balance aging, with overdue amounts flagged
  • Waitlist with days since last contact
  • Practice dashboard with monthly revenue

This is a spreadsheet template. It is not an EHR, and it is not a HIPAA compliance solution. You are responsible for how you store and protect client information.
